Design a modern fintech dashboard prototype for an AI-powered Corporate Credit Decisioning System called "CredVista".

The system is used by bank credit managers to evaluate corporate loan applications using AI.

Create a professional enterprise banking interface with a clean fintech design similar to Stripe or modern banking dashboards.

Main Workflow Screens:

1. Login Page
- Secure login for bank credit officers
- Branding: CredVista
- Tagline: "AI-Powered Corporate Credit Intelligence"

2. Dashboard
- Overview of loan applications
- Cards showing:
  - Total applications
  - High risk companies
  - Pending review
  - Approved loans
- Recent company evaluations list
- AI risk alerts panel

3. Data Upload Screen (Data Ingestor)
- Upload multiple documents
  - GST filings
  - Bank statements
  - Annual reports (PDF)
  - Financial statements
- Drag-and-drop upload area
- File validation indicators
- AI parsing progress animation

4. AI Analysis Screen
- Show AI extracting insights from documents
- Panels for:
  - Financial analysis
  - Document risk analysis
  - Legal risk detection
  - Sector news insights
- Risk indicators (Low / Medium / High)

5. Research Agent Screen
- AI-discovered insights:
  - News related to company
  - Promoter background
  - Litigation risks
  - Industry trends
- Timeline view of detected events

6. Credit Risk Scoring Screen
- Show the Five Cs of Credit:
  - Character
  - Capacity
  - Capital
  - Collateral
  - Conditions
- AI scoring visualization (radar chart or score cards)

7. Recommendation Screen
- Final AI decision:
  - Approve / Reject / Review
- Suggested loan amount
- Suggested interest rate
- Risk explanation

8. CAM Generator Screen
- Auto-generated Credit Appraisal Memo
- Professional report preview
- Sections:
  - Company Overview
  - Financial Summary
  - Risk Analysis
  - AI Recommendation
- Export buttons:
  - Download PDF
  - Download Word

Design Requirements:
- Professional fintech style
- Light background with blue accent colors
- Clean banking UI
- Graphs and analytics widgets
- Risk score visualizations
- Modern SaaS dashboard layout

Target users: Indian bank credit managers analyzing corporate loan applications.

Make it look like a production-ready AI banking platform.
